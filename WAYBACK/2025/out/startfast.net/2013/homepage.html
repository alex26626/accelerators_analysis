StartFast! Software, Internet & Mobile Accelerator

StartFast is now accepting applications for Summer 2013. APPLY NOW
If you’ve applied to another accelerator on accelerato.rs click HERE.

Three months to get focused, get feedback, and get funded. We’ll give you enough money to pursue your software/internet startup full-time, co-working space, resources to work with, and continuous feedback on your progress from mentors who’ve done it before. Post-program, our mission is to help you get the funding and resources needed to scale and achieve a successful exit.

We’re not just another accelerator.  In addition to exceptional, globally connected mentors and program leaders, we offer our teams:

  • Up to $18,000 in cash and over $900,000 in perks
  • Opportunity to compete for $300,000 (and growing) post-program funding
  • The chance to work with our Entrepreneurs in Residence as virtual members of your startup team
  • Access to high quality, affordable talent;
  • Ability to achieve your goals not just faster, but with an ultra low burn rate;
  • Our successful track record of getting companies funded;
  • Support from our emerging entrepreneurial community;
  • And much more…

StartFast welcomes applicants from all over the United States and around the world. Contact us to be a StartFast mentor, sponsor, investor or volunteer.
