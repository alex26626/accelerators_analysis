Capital Innovators
Capital Innovators is an innovative program offering $50,000 in seed money to tech companies in St. Louis. The rigorous twelve-week program includes FREE office-space, FREE legal and marketing services, subsidized housing in downtown St. Louis, FREE wi-fi and web hosting and access to the best and brightest that St. Louis has to offer through the university fellowship program and mentor program. The program concludes with the opportunity to demo your IT company to major investors who can take your business to the next level!
