Tech Wildcatters is a mentor driven technology start-up accelerator lead by entrepreneurs.
Here at Tech Wildcatters, we know what we’re good at and we know where we can add value. Dallas is a business town plain and simple. If you have a web, gaming or software startup that receives some or all of its revenue from businesses, then you are probably right for us.
We are looking for smart capable teams of 2 or more entrepreneurs who can execute. Each chosen company will receive $20-25K in seed capital and access to our remarkable mentors and sponsors, all focused on getting your company to the next level.
