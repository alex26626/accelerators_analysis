AlphaLab is a Pittsburgh-based accelerator program designed to build and launch innovative startup companies. AlphaLab is a program of Innovation Works, one of the most active seed-stage investors in the nation, and a charter member of the Global Accelerator Network.

As a program of Innovation Works, AlphaLab is supported by the Pennsylvania Department of Community and Economic Development.
