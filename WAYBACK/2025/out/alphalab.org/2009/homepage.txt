Welcome to AlphaLab
AlphaLab is a catalyst for launching the next generation of software, entertainment technology and Internet-related companies. Created by
Innovation Works
, one of the nation’s most active seed-stage investors, AlphaLab provides funding, hands-on business assistance, access to a network of entrepreneurs and expert advisors, and free office space as part of an intensive 20-week program in Pittsburgh. AlphaLab helps companies rapidly develop their technology, gain user feedback from early product releases, develop go-to-market strategies, and move toward successful commercial launch.
AlphaLab began its Summer/Fall program with its first six companies on June 2, 2008.  The application deadline for the Winter/Spring program was October 30, 2008.
