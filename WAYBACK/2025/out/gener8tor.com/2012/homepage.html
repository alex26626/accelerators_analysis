Startup Weekend Coming Soon!

Startup Weekend is an intense 54 hour event which focuses on building a web or mobile application which could form the basis of a credible business over the course of a weekend. The weekend brings together people with different skillsets - primarily software developers, graphics designers and business people - to build applications and develop a commercial case around them. Startup Weekend Madison will take place the weekend of April 27-29, 2012 and will be held at the Madison College - West Campus.
