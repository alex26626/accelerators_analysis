About Us


The Desai Accelerator is a collaborative effort at the University of Michigan between the Zell Lurie Institute of Entrepreneurial Studies at the Ross School of Business and the Center for Entrepreneurship at the College of Engineering. The Desai Accelerator is made possible through the Desai Sethi Family Foundation, Davidson Foundation, and the Michigan Economic Development Corporation.

While the accelerator is a University of Michigan supported program, it is open to anyone to apply.
