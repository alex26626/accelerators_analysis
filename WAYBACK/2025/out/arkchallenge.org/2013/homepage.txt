The ARK Comes to NWA
Producer Iti Agnihotri Mudholkar of Ozarks at Large, a program of KUAF 91.3 FM National Public Radio, covers the official launching of The ARK
The Iceberg Coworking Space
All participants of The ARK will receive 24x7 access to The Iceberg, the new regional coworking facility in downtown Fayetteville, Arkansas.
Who
We are a group of advisors, entrepreneurs, key cluster experts and investors interested in economic development through the mentorship and acceleration of young technology startups.
What
A full member of the Global Accelerator Network, The ARK is a new mentorship-driven business accelerator program for technology startups serving the retail, transportation and logistics and food industries.
When
The final deadline is April 14, 2013, at 11:59 pm CST. 

The ARK bootcamp will be from June to mid-September 2013 in Fayetteville, Arkansas.
Where
The ARK bootcamp will take place in Northwest Arkansas with the base being at The Iceberg Coworking Space in Fayetteville.
