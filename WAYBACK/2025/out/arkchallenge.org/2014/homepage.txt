The ARK Challenge Expands to Two Programs in 2014
$50K each to 5 Companies in NWA and $20K each to 10 Companies in Central
Who
We are a group of advisors, entrepreneurs, key cluster experts and investors interested in economic development through mentorship and acceleration of growing technology startups.
What
A full member of the Global Accelerator Network, The ARK is a new mentorship-driven business accelerator program for technology startups serving the retail/logistics industries for Northwest Arkansas and finance IT/health IT/government services in Central Arkansas.
When
The final deadline for Summer 2014 is May 12, 2014, at 11:59 pm CST. The ARK Challenge Summer 2014 program will be from mid-June to mid-September 2014 in Fayetteville, Arkansas, USA.
The final deadline for Fall 2014 is July 8, 2014, at 11:59 pm CST. The ARK Challenge Fall 2014 program will be from early August to early November 2014 in Little Rock, Arkansas, USA.
Where
The ARK Summer 2014 bootcamp will take place in Northwest Arkansas with the base being on the second floor of East Square Plaza on the Downtown Fayetteville Square.
The ARK Fall 2014 bootcamp will take place in Central Arkansas with the base being in Downtown Little Rock.
