Straight Shot is an accelerator dedicated to rapidly developing technology startups in a way that will allow them to succeed the first time.
We connect passionate entrepreneurs with expert mentors and invested sponsors to provide their startups with all the tools they need to succeed. Not only when they’re ready to go to market, but also in their search for funding on the way there.
Entrepreneurs
Straight Shot provides entrepreneurs in ecommerce and software as a service models with a direct path to success. Our founders and expert mentors have first-hand experience in creating successful businesses in the industry, and offer unmatched perspective and knowledge about how you can do the same.
Mentors
At Straight Shot, mentorship isn't a one-way street. Entrepreneurs gain keen insight and guidance by working directly with industry experts. Mentors come away reenergized through the opportunity to work together with incredible new ideas and talent as well as other experts.
