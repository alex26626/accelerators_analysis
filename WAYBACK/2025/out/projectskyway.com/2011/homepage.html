  • ENTREPRENEURS

    “The Opportunity And Support Needed To Succeed”

    Project Skyway is a tech accelerator for motivated software entrepreneurs launching Summer 2011.  We are helping 10 early-stage software companies to start smart by offering them seed money, mentorship, co-working space and connections.

  • FRIENDS

    “Growing The Tech Community In Minnesota”

    Project Skyway is Minnesota’s first tech accelerator program.  We are committed to improving early-stage technology in Minnesota.  Project Skyway helps early-stage tech companies by providing them mentorship, seed capital and a home in Minnesota for their company.

  • INVESTORS

    “Connecting Ideas, Innovation, Investment & Entrepreneurs”

    Project Skyway is focused on  building companies with long-term, sustainable value, ethical practices, mentorship, and strong networks.  We connect entrepreneurs with business leaders and to each other creating an ecosystem that cultivates ideas.
