Project Skyway™ is a tech accelerator for motivated software entrepreneurs.  Twice a year the program will help 10 early-stage software companies by offering seed money, resources and connections to entrepreneurs, mentors and investors.
FRIENDS
“Growing
The

Community
In
Minnesota”
Project Skyway™ is Minnesota’s first tech accelerator program.  We are committed to improving early-stage technology in Minnesota.  Project Skyway helps early-stage tech companies by providing mentorship, seed capital and a home in Minnesota for their company.

Project Skyway™ is focused on  building companies with long-term, sustainable value, ethical practices, mentorship, and strong networks.  We connect entrepreneurs with business leaders and to each other creating an ecosystem that cultivates ideas.
