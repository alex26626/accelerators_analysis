The Program
Eight to ten growth-stage digital health companies that are developing cutting edge technology products for healthcare providers and patients will be accepted into the 5-month Accelerator class.
About the Program
Mentor Network
The companies will receive direct access to customers and product development feedback from senior-level executives with over a dozen leading healthcare provider organizations in New York.
