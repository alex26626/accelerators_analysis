LearnLaunchX is a startup accelerator based in Boston, MA focused on enabling education technology entrepreneurs to build and grow their startups into thriving companies.

LearnLaunchX fosters and drives innovation by providing startups seed funding, a place to work, an unmatched team of mentors with deep ed tech experience and relationships and direct access to the learning marketplace with the goal of creating successful businesses

LearnLaunchX's first accelerator class in Boston begins on June 10, 2013.
