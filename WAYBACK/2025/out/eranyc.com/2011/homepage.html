Get your funding started.
Get $25K in funding for your startup! At the end of ER Accelerator's 3 month session, pitch at Demo Day to the most prominent early stage investors in the tech community. 

We know New York Tech. 
Connect with an amazing group of mentors in the world’s most vibrant city. ER Accelerator brings together more than 140 mentors including leading entrepreneurs, operators, functional experts, and investors to help your company succeed. 

Hands on help. 
Each startup gets one-on-one support from ER Accelerator's operating partners and mentors, as well as access to industry specialists, service providers, interns and resident technologists to reach their goals. From testing a product to validating with consumers or jump-starting sales, our mentors have the know-how to help get your startup off the ground.
