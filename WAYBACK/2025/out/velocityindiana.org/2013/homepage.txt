Plans & pricing
The team’s at Velocity will take part in a program based on the Lean LaunchPad approach to building startup companies.
Crafting a Hypothesis
Teams will apply the scientific method to there business model by breaking it down into an individual hypothesis
Test & Experiment
Teams will build a simple and inexpensive experiment. Then we
get out of the building,
talking to customers & partners to test & validate our assumptions.
Response & Iteration
If (& when) the hypothesis fails, we pivot the business model and repeat the process until the entire model is proven; no longer based on extensive assumptions.
The Lean LaunchPad was popularized by Steve Blank and is taught at Standford, UC Berkley and other Universities across the US. It has been applied to accelerator programs, the NSF, intrapeneurship programs, as well as high school and middle schools students. The approach enables a startup to build and validate that they have a viable business model before they attempt to build and scale a company. This is done using Alexander Osterwalder’s Business Model Canvas as the means to document the model. It further employs Blank’s Customer Development approach to translate business model assumptions into facts by running experiments.
This approach is not a one size fits all approach. It is applied in a different and unique way to each company, depending on their industry, level of development and experience. It is also not a prescriptive methodology, but rather a general approach that applies the principles learned over the past two decades on how to build successful, scalable companies.
