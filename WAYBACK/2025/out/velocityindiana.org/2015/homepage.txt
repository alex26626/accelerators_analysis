BUILD A GREAT BUSINESS
You have a great product, we have a 100-day program to show you how to build a great business. Velocity’s accelerator program will teach you how to discover, develop and close customers, build strategic partnerships and raise money to take your idea to the next stage of growth. Here are a few things that founders talk about after their Velocity experience.
DIRECTION
You will become a master of the Business Model Canvas and will present your challenges and victories in front of mentors and peers weekly.
METRICS
We help you develop a single metric that matters most to your business and you will work with us on a weekly basis to uncover ways to improve it.
MENTORSHIP
Learn the basics of building a business, from developing a value proposition that customers care about to making your first hire, to negotiating strategic partnerships.
COMMUNITY
The entrepreneurial journey is lonely. Velocity is a hub of individuals who understand what you’re going through because they have chosen the same path as you.
