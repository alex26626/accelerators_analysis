The Telluride Venture Accelerator is the first accelerator focused on outdoor recreation, tourism, natural products, health, energy, water, and education.
We accept three businesses each year and become a partner in your success.
We invest $30,000 (plus up to an additional $8000 for transportation and lodging), surround you with world-class mentoring, and assist with getting follow-on investment.
The Telluride community includes dozens of successful, experienced entrepreneurs eager to lend their expertise as your
mentor
. The Telluride Venture Accelerator also offers an
entrepreneur-in-residence
program that brings in high profile entrepreneurs for dedicated mentoring. Specific experts will also be invited as part of our
custom mentoring
program for participating businesses.
