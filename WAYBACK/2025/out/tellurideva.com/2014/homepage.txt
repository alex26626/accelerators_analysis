The Telluride Venture Accelerator is the first accelerator focused on outdoor recreation, tourism, natural products, health, energy, water, and education.
Each year we accept up to six businesses, and become a partner in your success.
We invest $25,000, surround you with world-class mentoring, and assist with getting follow-on investment.
Applications for 2014 are now closed.
The Telluride Venture Accelerator’s greatest strength is its incredible community of
mentors
. This group includes dozens of successful, experienced entrepreneurs eager to lend their expertise to improving your business. TVA also has an
entrepreneur-in-residence
program that brings in high profile entrepreneurs for dedicated mentoring. Specific experts will also be invited as part of our custom mentoring program for participating businesses.
