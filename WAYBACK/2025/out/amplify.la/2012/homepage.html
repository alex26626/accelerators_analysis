We amplify startups by providing seed funding, access to additional capital, and an unmatched team of mentors and advisors who have a unique connection to media and entertainment. Every company accepted will receive up to $50K in seed funding and free workspace in our community entrepreneur facility in Venice.

Our companies will also take part in a highly organized mentorship curriculum designed to ensure your team gets the specific guidance and area expertise when it’s most important. Our program is pragmatic and intended to create lasting value for your company. In other words, we aren't building an incubation machine; Amplify is a nurturing engine that will make you better entrepreneurs and make your companies more valuable. That's a promise.

Amplify is recruiting the most talented, hard working and visionary entrepreneurs to participate in our 4 month accelerator program. We aren't the same old accelerator, so don't be the same old startup.

We feature a rolling curriculum, companies can apply and be admitted at any time. In addition to traditional foundational support and discounted services, Amplify members will receive:

  • Up to $50k in seed capital and the opportunity for follow-on support
  • Dedicated office space with VIP access to community workshops and events
  • Hands-on, weekly mentor meetings with seasoned entrepreneurs
  • 3-year hiatus on city taxes.

Amplify itself is a startup, run by an incredible team of entrepreneurs and visionaries. We expect to benefit from the community we’re building as much as our members will. For us, making a real impact in the Los Angeles startup ecosystem is at the heart of our strategy.

  1. Our physical space is a real community - not a room with desks

  2. Our mentor program is highly organized and pragmatic - they will spend real time with you

  3. Our management team works “in” the companies - not investors looking to increase their wealth on your back

  4. We are tightly connected to media and entertainment in Los Angeles - not hustlers who know the usual suspects

  5. Our investor network is extraordinary in LA and far beyond - not just a few big names
