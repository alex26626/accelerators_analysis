Rock Health
is the first seed-accelerator for health apps.  By bringing together the brightest minds in technology and healthcare, Rock Health provides crucial funding, mentorship and operational support to high-potential, early stage start-ups, nurturing the next generation of the digital health ecosystem.  We believe in creative, tech-driven solutions and are located in San Francisco, CA.
