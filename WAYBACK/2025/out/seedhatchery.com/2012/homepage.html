The innovative ideas poured in, and now the announcement you’ve been waiting for is almost here! At the end of this month, we’ll announce the 2012 Seed Hatchery cohort of tech-based companies who will launch right here in the Mid-South.

 

Applications for the six slots were submitted from far and wide, including California, China, Chile, and Italy. Selected founding teams will kick off their 90-day marine-style bootcamp on January 28. Be sure to check back soon for more information and updates on the 2012 Seed Hatchery Cohort!
