Information Session Moved to January 18th

We will now host our application information session on Tuesday the 18th at 5:30pm at EmergeMemphis. If you have an questions about Seed Hatchery or our application then we encourage you to attend this session.

We encourage you to apply early for our first cohort. Our application deadline is approaching fast and those that apply early have a distinct advantage over those that wait to the last minute –the advantage being that we can get to know you better and ask more questions to learn more.

If you have any questions be sure to reach out to us via our contact us page.

Application Deadline: February 11th, 2011

Posted on December 8, 2010 by SeedHatchery

Application Deadline: February 11th, 2011

Applications are now open for our Spring 2011 cohort. While there is no set criteria for the type of idea will we select, we admit that our level of funding is usually most suitable for technology-based products and services. Our open call for applications will close on February 11th, 2011, but we strongly encourage you to apply early — it gives us more time to get to know you, likely increasing your changes of getting into our 90 day cohort program that includes investments of capital and mentorship.

If you have the right idea and the passion to see it launch, we invite you to submit your application.

About Us: Seed Hatchery is a mentorship-driven seed stage investment program. We support emerging technology entrepreneurs with $15,000 in capital and strategic mentors to sharpen ideas in to strong startups. In return for this investment, Seed Hatchery and our mentors collectively receive a 10% ownership stake in the Common Stock of the company.
