Startup Accelerator
XLerateHealth offers an
intensive 12-week training course
that connects your startup to world-class
Mentors
and leading health and wellness companies in the United States. Participants will have continued exposure to potential customers,  partners, and thought-leaders. Our Mentors are the backbone of our program and offer a network that can dramatically impact the trajectory of your business. The program is a combination of classroom time, one-on-one sessions with Mentors, and focused customer discovery.

Why Your Startup Should Apply to XLerateHealth
XLerateHealth’s Mentor base, our access to customers and pilot sites, and our extensive experience in building successful startups makes our program a leader among healthcare accelerators. You should apply to XLerateHealth because our focus is on getting you face to face with customers as quickly as possible. You will leave this program with much more than a pitch deck – you’ll leave XLerateHealth with a viable business.
What We’re Looking For
We are looking for dynamic teams of entrepreneurs who are passionate about what they do and are interested in creating disruptive solutions to tough problems in the healthcare ecosystem. We prefer teams that include founders with technical ability, a knowledge of the healthcare industry, and who are intent on succeeding as scaleable commercial enterprises. We expect all key team members to participate in Louisville for the duration of the program.
Application Process and Requirements
We are accepting
online
applications until
May 31, 2014.
After an initial review of all applications, we will contact select companies to participate in a web conference. Our review period will continue throughout May, and we will begin notifying selected companies in June.
What Selected Teams Get
A $20,000 stipend
Support from XLerateHealth’s
Mentor
network, as well as introductions and meetings with pilot customers, potential funders and potential acquirers.
Free office space in the heart of Louisville’s downtown within walking distance to the NuLu district’s bars, coffee shops, restaurants and meeting/co-working spaces.
What XLerateHealth Gets
In exchange for the funding, support, and mentorship, XLerateHealth receives a 6% equity stake in your company. If you do not require the $20,000 stipend, XLerate Health will receive a 2% equity stake. Like the founders, XLerateHealth receives common stock.
Your Intellectual Property is Safe
Your application will only be viewed by the XLerateHealth selection team and will remain confidential.
