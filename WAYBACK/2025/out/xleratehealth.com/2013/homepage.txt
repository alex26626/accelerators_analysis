Startup Accelerator
XLerateHealth offers an
intensive 10-week training course
that connects your startup to world-class
Mentors
and leading health and wellness companies in the United States. Participants in the program will have continued exposure to potential customers and funders, as well as thought-leaders in the transformation of the health delivery system. These Mentors are the backbone of our program and offer a network that can dramatically impact the trajectory of your business. The program is a combination of classroom time, scheduled one-on-one sessions with Mentors, and focused customer discovery.

Why Your Startup Should Apply to XLerateHealth
There are a lot of great accelerators out there, including a select number that focus on health and wellness. We believe that XLerateHealth’s Mentor base, our access to customers and pilot sites, and our extensive experience in building successful startups makes our program a leader among healthcare accelerators. You should apply to XLerateHealth because our focus is on getting you face to face with customers as quickly as possible. You will leave this program with much more than a pitch deck; you’ll leave XLerateHealth with a viable business.
What We’re Looking For
We are looking for dynamic teams of entrepreneurs who are passionate about what they do and are interested in creating disruptive solutions to tough problems in the healthcare ecosystem. We prefer teams that include founders with technical ability, a knowledge of the healthcare industry, and who are intent on succeeding as scaleable commercial enterprises.
Application Process and Requirements
We will start with an
online application
. From there we will select companies we would like to participate in a web conference. We then will have an in-person interview as the final step. We will work through this process quickly and welcome questions at any point. The only requirement is that the majority of your team be present in Louisville for the duration of the program.

What XLerateHealth Gets
In exchange for the funding, support and Mentorship, XLerateHealth receives a 6% equity stake in your company. If you do not require the $20,000 stipend, your company may participate in the accelerator program in exchange for a significantly smaller equity stake. Like the founders, XLerateHealth receives common stock.
Your Intellectual Property is Safe
Your application will only be viewed by the XLerateHealth selection team and will remain confidential.
