Our Founder

Bizdom is one of the most connected startup accelerators in the world! We were founded by serial entrepreneur Dan Gilbert, Founder and Chairman of Quicken Loans and Rock Ventures.

Dan created Bizdom to help provide the support and resources necessary for aspiring entrepreneurs to get their businesses off the ground in Detroit and Cleveland. Bizdom is a nonprofit organization. One hundred percent of the returns on our investments are recycled back into Detroit and Cleveland to launch more startups!
