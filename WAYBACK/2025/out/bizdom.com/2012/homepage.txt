THE DEAL
During our three-month accelerator, entrepreneurs receive up to
$25k in seed funding, active and hands on mentoring, access to our family of companies and a dynamic, engaging, and collaborative workspace. By the end of the three months, those who are ready can pitch for additional funding from multiple investors at our DEMO DAY.

OUR FOUNDER
Bizdom is a non-profit, founded by serial entrepreneur Dan Gilbert, Founder and Chairman of Quicken Loans, Majority owner of the Cleveland Cavaliers, and General Partner at Detroit Venture Partners.
Dan created Bizdom to help provide the support and resources necessary for the best and brightest aspiring entrepreneurs to get their businesses off the ground in Detroit and Cleveland.
