Incubate
A vision and a prototype will only get you so far.
Highway1 helps you build a business.
Our four month program supplies you with:
• A dedicated staff of experienced engineers ­
• An inside view into the supply chain and manufacturing infrastructure in China
­• An education in manufacturing, supply chain, and distribution as well as project and business management, finance, and strategy
­• Office & machine shop space in San Francisco
­• Mentorship and guidance from across the tech sector
­• Fine-tune your pitch for potential investors­
• Access to an extensive network of investors
• Seed Funding of $50,000 in return for 4-7% equity
We’re looking for small teams that want to build hardware companies for the long term. If this describes you, let us know.
