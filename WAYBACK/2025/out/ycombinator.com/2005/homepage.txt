Y Combinator
is a new kind of venture firm specializing in
funding early stage startups.  We help startups through
what is for many the hardest step, from idea to company.
We invest mostly in computer-related ideas.
And because we are ourselves technology people, 
we prefer groups with a lot of technical depth.
We care more about how smart you are than how old
you are, and more about the quality of your idea
than whether you have a formal business plan.
