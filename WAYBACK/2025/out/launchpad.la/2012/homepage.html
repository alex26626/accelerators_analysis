Who We Are

Launchpad LA is a startup accelerator and mentorship organization designed to help young entrepreneurs based in Los Angeles get the guidance and relationships needed to help take their companies to the next level. It combines the best VCs in Southern California, many experienced CEOs and a very select group of service providers who are committed to working with early-stage companies.

Launchpad LA is currently accepting applications for its fourth class of tech startups. The application deadline is June 21st and the class will run from July until the end of November. Each company that is selected will receive $50,000 in funding, free office space, and access to our world-class network of mentors and advisors.

If you are interested in becoming more involved with Launchpad LA, let us know!
